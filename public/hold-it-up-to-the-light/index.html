Stop 24. The Gift.

The flash goes through the ribbon, the paper, the tape, the box, the tissue, the second box (no comment), the bubble wrap and the velvet. At the very middle there is a small, sky-blue crystal.

Celestine. Case 24. Missing since 1911. It was never missing. It came by post. It was in here. The tag says “For C.”, and C. was never a who.

Then it charges. It works far too well. Celestine throws the light straight back up through the skylight, and at three in the morning the whole town looks up and sees the inside of the gift drawn on the clouds.

The gift that hated surprises is now the biggest surprise the town has ever had. It finds, to its own surprise, that it doesn't mind. That concludes the tour.

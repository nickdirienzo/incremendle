Read Me

Under the last of the mud, painted across the Tremendous in letters a yard high:

THANK YOU, FLOWER! SAME AGAIN NEXT YEAR? · L.K.C.

L.K.C. is the Lowmarsh Kite Club. Every autumn they fly their old kites at Tumble Hill on purpose, because the Tramontane always carries them up, and buy the clean scrap back off the Exchange to build new ones. New silk is expensive. Recycled silk, graded by a flower, is not.

All of them are coming up the hill now, with this year's kites, to sit in the folding chairs. They would like to hear the seminar.

“I knew they would come. It was all in my vision. / Take notes, little ones: wilting's a decision.”
